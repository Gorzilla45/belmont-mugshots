===============================================
  HOW TO PUT THIS APP ONLINE
  (so you can open it on your phone)
  Using Render.com – free
===============================================

This takes about 10–15 minutes the first time.
After that you just use the link on your phone.

-----------------------------------------------
STEP 1 – Create a free Render account
-----------------------------------------------

1. On your computer or phone, go to:
   https://render.com

2. Click “Get Started” or “Sign Up”.

3. Sign up with your Google account or email
   (Google is easiest).

4. Confirm your email if asked.

-----------------------------------------------
STEP 2 – Put the app files on GitHub (free)
-----------------------------------------------

Render needs the files on GitHub.

Easiest way:

1. Go to https://github.com and sign up
   (or log in if you already have an account).

2. Click the “+” in the top right → “New repository”.

3. Name it:  belmont-mugshots
   Leave everything else as default.
   Click “Create repository”.

4. On the next page, click
   “uploading an existing file”.

5. Drag ALL the files from the
   belmont_mugshot_app folder into the
   upload area (app.py, requirements.txt,
   Procfile, templates folder, etc.).

6. Click “Commit changes”.

-----------------------------------------------
STEP 3 – Connect the app to Render
-----------------------------------------------

1. Go back to https://render.com and log in.

2. Click “New +” → “Web Service”.

3. Click “Connect a repository”
   and choose the “belmont-mugshots”
   repository you just created.
   (You may need to give Render permission
   the first time.)

4. Fill in these settings:

   Name:          belmont-mugshots
   Region:        (leave default)
   Branch:        main
   Runtime:       Python
   Build Command: pip install -r requirements.txt
   Start Command: gunicorn app:app

5. Click “Create Web Service”.

6. Wait 2–5 minutes while it builds.
   You will see logs scrolling.
   When it says “Live” or “Deploy live”,
   you are done.

-----------------------------------------------
STEP 4 – Get your phone link
-----------------------------------------------

On the Render page for your service you will
see a URL that looks like:

   https://belmont-mugshots.onrender.com

That is your permanent link.

- Bookmark it on your phone.
- Open it any time to check for new inmates
  and copy posts.

-----------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT NOTES
-----------------------------------------------

• Free Render services “sleep” after about
  15 minutes of no one using them.
  The first time you open the link after
  it has slept, it may take 30–60 seconds
  to wake up. Just wait.

• The automatic hourly check only works
  while the service is awake.
  On free plan you will mostly click the
  “Check for New Inmates Now” button
  yourself when you open the page.

• The app is public (anyone with the link
  can see it). Do not put private passwords
  in it.
